# container_pics

Photographs of the container builds.

Anything placed in this folder is published with the site and is publicly reachable at
`https://radhouses.eu/container_pics/<filename>`. Nothing on the site links to it, but the
files are not private — don't put anything here that shouldn't be on the open internet.

Nothing here is used by the site yet. To put one of these on a page, follow the conventions
in [`../images/README.md`](../images/README.md) — WebP, under 200 KB, long edge 1600 px.
